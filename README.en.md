## About me

I am Pedro Yada, a second-year student in the technical high school programming program at Germinare Tech and an intern at PicPay, a Brazilian digital bank, where I work on data analysis within the Strategic Planning team of the Insurance business unit.

My development focus is building **AI systems and backend APIs**, connecting agents, tools, and databases to solve practical problems.

I have hands-on experience building multi-agent systems with **LangChain and LangGraph**, including tools, guardrails, judge agents, FAQ systems, and RAG. I have also worked with **Qdrant** for semantic search, **MongoDB** for conversation memory and sessions, and **PostgreSQL** in agent integrations.

I want to pursue a career in AI Engineering while deepening my knowledge of software development, infrastructure, and AI system evaluation.

I am also interested in exploring **cybersecurity** as a new area of study.
